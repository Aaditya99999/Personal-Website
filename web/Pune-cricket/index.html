Our 10-Year Journey

12 Years of Building Pune's Cricket Legacy

Founded in 2014, Pune Cricket Academy has spent over a decade building a professional, accessible, and world-class cricket training environment for young players across Pune.

What began as a small group of passionate coaches and 8 students at Shivajinagar has evolved into one of Pune's most respected cricket academies — now with 30+ active students, 5 expert coaches, and hundreds of alumni who've gone on to compete at district and state levels.

Every year, we refine our methods, upgrade our facilities, and deepen our commitment to helping every student — regardless of level — reach their potential.
