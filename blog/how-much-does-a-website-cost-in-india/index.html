Website Pricing

How Much Does a Website Cost in India?

Website cost depends less on hype and more on scope. A local business does not need the same setup as a full software product or a large e-commerce build.

What affects website cost?

The number of pages, custom features, design complexity, forms, integrations, and content support all affect the final price. A small business website usually needs a focused structure instead of a long list of expensive extras.

What does a small business actually need?

For many businesses, the essentials are simple: a strong homepage, service sections, about information, trust signals, contact options, a WhatsApp button, and basic SEO setup. That is why a starter website can often fit under Rs 10,000.

When cost goes up

Price increases when the website needs advanced booking systems, user accounts, complex CMS requirements, custom dashboards, or heavy integrations. Those are valid needs, but not every business needs them from day one.

How to avoid overpaying

  • Start with the pages that support the main business offer
  • Do not overbuild before you validate the need
  • Make sure mobile usability and lead capture are included
  • Choose someone who understands both SEO and conversion
