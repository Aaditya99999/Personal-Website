Frequently Asked

Before You Reach Out

If your question is not answered here, the simplest path is to call or WhatsApp directly. Every matter is different, and a brief conversation is often more useful than any written list of answers.

How do I book a consultation with Adv Narendra Katariya?

+

The simplest way to begin is by calling or WhatsApp-ing directly on +91 99508 18437. Share your name, a brief description of your matter, and your preferred time. A consultation will be arranged promptly.

You may also use the enquiry form on the Consultation page — your submission will be followed up within one business day. For urgent matters, direct call or WhatsApp is strongly recommended.

Where is the office located?

+

The chamber is located at the District Court premises, Sikar, Rajasthan. If you plan to visit in person, please confirm your appointment by call or WhatsApp in advance to ensure availability.

Walk-in visits may not always be possible due to prior court engagements. A prior call takes only a moment and ensures you are seen without delay.

Does Adv Katariya handle cases in Sikar and Rajasthan?

+

Yes. The primary jurisdiction is Sikar District Court, Rajasthan, where Adv Narendra Katariya is a regular and recognised presence. Matters in the district are handled across civil, criminal, family, and documentation areas.

If your matter involves another jurisdiction within Rajasthan, please mention this during the consultation — guidance will be provided on the most appropriate course of action for your specific situation.

Can I contact via WhatsApp?

+

Yes. WhatsApp is a convenient and welcome way to make first contact. You can reach Adv Narendra Katariya directly at +91 99508 18437.

When messaging via WhatsApp, briefly describe your matter and your preferred time for a consultation. Please avoid sharing highly sensitive details over messaging at the initial stage — these are best discussed in a private consultation.

What details should I prepare before the consultation?

+

For a productive first consultation, it is helpful to have the following ready:

— A clear, chronological account of the matter (what happened, when, who is involved)
— Any relevant documents (court notices, agreements, property papers, police complaints, etc.)
— A list of any questions you would like answered

Do not worry if your documents are incomplete or your account is unclear — the consultation is precisely the place to sort through these things. Come with whatever you have and the rest can be gathered as needed.

Is the initial consultation confidential?

+

Yes, completely. All consultations — and everything shared within them — are held in strict confidence. Nothing disclosed during a consultation is shared with any third party.

This confidentiality applies regardless of whether you choose to proceed further. You may speak openly without concern that your matter will be discussed beyond the chamber.

Am I obligated to proceed after the first consultation?

+

No. A consultation carries no obligation of any kind. Its purpose is to help you understand your legal position and your options — nothing more. Any decision about how to proceed, if at all, rests entirely with you.

Many clients find that even a single consultation gives them the clarity they need to make an informed decision, whether or not they choose to engage legal representation.

What types of matters are handled?

+

The practice covers civil matters (property disputes, contract issues, civil rights), criminal matters (defence, bail, advisory), family and matrimonial matters (disputes, maintenance, custody considerations), legal documentation (agreements, affidavits, declarations), and court representation at Sikar District Court.

If you are unsure whether your matter falls within these areas, simply reach out — a brief conversation is the quickest way to find out. Visit the Practice Areas page for full details.

How long does a typical matter take to resolve?

+

The duration of a legal matter depends entirely on its nature, complexity, and the specific circumstances involved. Simple documentation matters may be resolved quickly, while court proceedings can take considerably longer depending on the stage of the case and court schedules.

During the initial consultation, a realistic estimate will be provided based on your specific situation. Honest timelines — not optimistic ones — are always given, because managing expectations correctly is part of responsible legal counsel.
