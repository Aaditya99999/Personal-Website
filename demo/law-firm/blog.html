How much does a lawyer cost in India?

+

Legal fees in India vary widely depending on the type of case, complexity, and the lawyer's experience. For routine matters like documentation or basic consultations, fees can start from ₹500–₹2,000. For court representation, fees may range from ₹5,000 to several lakhs depending on the case.

How long does a legal case take to resolve?

+

Case duration varies significantly. Simple matters like document drafting can be done in days. Criminal bail applications are often heard within 1–7 days. However, contested cases in court can take months to years.

  • Bail hearing: 1–7 days
  • Mutual divorce: 6–18 months
  • Contested divorce: 2–5 years
  • Property dispute: 2–7 years
  • Contract dispute: 1–4 years

We always work to resolve matters as quickly as possible through negotiation, mediation, or efficient litigation strategy.

What is anticipatory bail and who can apply?

+

Anticipatory bail (under Section 438 of CrPC) allows a person to apply for bail in anticipation of an arrest. If you have reason to believe you may be arrested in connection with a non-bailable offence, you can apply to the Sessions Court or High Court for anticipatory bail.

Once granted, if police arrest you, you can be released immediately on bail. We file anticipatory bail applications urgently. Contact us today if you're facing this situation.

How do I file for divorce in India?

+

Divorce in India depends on the religion and marriage act applicable. Generally:

  • Mutual Consent Divorce: Both parties agree. Filed under Sec 13B HMA. Takes 6–18 months.
  • Contested Divorce: One party files on grounds like cruelty, desertion, adultery, etc. Takes longer.

How is child custody decided after divorce?

+

Indian courts decide custody based primarily on the "best interest of the child." Key factors include:

  • Child's age (young children often stay with mother)
  • Financial stability of each parent
  • Emotional bond with the child
  • Child's own preference (if old enough)
  • Ability to provide education and stable environment

Both physical custody (who the child lives with) and legal custody (decision-making) can be awarded jointly or individually. We fight for your parental rights while always keeping the child's wellbeing central.

How do I verify property title before buying?

+

Before buying any property, you must verify:

  • Title search (ownership chain for past 30 years minimum)
  • Encumbrance certificate (any loans or charges on the property)
  • Approved building plans and completion certificate
  • No-objection certificates (NOC) if applicable
  • RERA registration for under-construction properties
  • Khata / mutation records in local authorities

Our property law team conducts thorough due diligence to protect your investment. We recommend never buying property without a legal title search — contact us before signing anything.

Do I need a lawyer to register a company?

+

While it's technically possible to register a company yourself, having a lawyer ensures accuracy, compliance, and avoids costly mistakes. Key tasks a lawyer handles include:

  • Choosing the right business structure (Pvt Ltd, LLP, Partnership, etc.)
  • Drafting Memorandum of Association (MoA) and Articles of Association (AoA)
  • Filing with Registrar of Companies (ROC)
  • Shareholder and partnership agreements
  • Compliance advisory and ongoing governance

Getting the legal foundation right from day one protects you from disputes and compliance issues later.
