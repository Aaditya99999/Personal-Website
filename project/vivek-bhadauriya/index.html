FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I book Vivek for a seminar at my college or school?

Choose "Seminar for school or college" in the form above and send it on WhatsApp, or call +91 63932 13527. Share your preferred dates and roughly how many students will attend.

What's the difference between the college and school seminars?

The college seminar is about LinkedIn: building a strong profile, a network and a personal brand for jobs and internships. The school seminar is about AI: using it to create websites, presentations and simple tools.

What does the digital marketing course cover?

Three areas: AI for marketing, performance marketing (Google and Meta ads), and core digital marketing like SEO, social media and Google Business. You also get AI tools and resources to keep.

Do you help with jobs and internships?

Yes. Students who take the course get job placement support, and can apply for paid and unpaid internships.

Can Vivek help my business grow?

Yes. Book a marketing consultancy for help with SEO, ads and leads, or a business growth consultancy for a wider plan to scale. Future Edge can also run the marketing for you.
