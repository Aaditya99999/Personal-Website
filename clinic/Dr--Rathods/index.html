FAQ

Common Questions

What are the clinic timings?+

We are open Monday to Saturday, from 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays. You can call us or book online anytime.

How do I book an appointment?+

Click the Book Appointment button on our website, call us directly, or visit the clinic in person. We'll confirm your slot within 24 hours.

Do you accept walk-in patients?+

Yes, we do accept walk-in patients subject to availability. We recommend booking in advance to avoid waiting and ensure the doctor is available for you.

Is the root canal treatment painful?+

Not at all! With modern anesthesia and advanced techniques, root canal treatment at Denta Care is virtually painless. Most patients are surprised by how comfortable it actually is.

What We Offer at
🦷
Teeth Cleaning
Preventive Care

Professional scaling and polishing remove plaque, tartar, and surface stains that regular brushing can't. Protects your gums, freshens breath, and keeps teeth healthy long-term. Recommended every 6 months.

😬
Braces & Teeth Alignment
Orthodontics

Modern metal and ceramic braces carefully fitted to straighten your teeth effectively. Our doctor designs a personalized treatment plan for optimal, lasting results.

💉
Root Canal Treatment
Restorative

Saves your natural tooth when the pulp is infected or damaged. Using precision instruments and anesthesia, the procedure is virtually painless. We clean, seal, and restore the tooth.

🔧
Tooth Extraction
Oral Surgery

When a tooth is severely damaged, decayed, or causing crowding, a gentle extraction is the safest solution. We ensure a comfortable, anxiety-free experience with proper aftercare guidance.

🔩
Dental Implants
Permanent Restoration

The gold standard for missing teeth — a titanium post fused with your jawbone, topped with a natural-looking crown. They look, feel, and function exactly like real teeth.

👄
Dentures
Prosthetic Dentistry

Comfortable, custom-fitted dentures for patients who have lost multiple or all teeth. Whether full or partial, we craft them to fit perfectly, restoring your ability to eat, speak, and smile naturally.
