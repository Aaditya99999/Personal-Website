7 Signs Your Business Website Needs a Redesign (and When It Doesn't)

A redesign is expensive and a website that works shouldn't be rebuilt just to look new. Here's how to tell which situation you're in.

By Aaditya Bhatnagar6 min readPublished September 17, 2026
Website RedesignConversionAudit

Your business website needs a redesign if it's slow or awkward on mobile, brings in almost no inquiries despite traffic, looks noticeably older than competitors, can't be updated without a developer, doesn't rank for your own services, has no clear call to action, or no longer matches what you sell. If only one or two of these apply, targeted fixes are usually cheaper than a full rebuild.

What are the 7 signs a website needs a redesign?

  1. It's slow on mobile. If PageSpeed Insights shows a poor mobile score and pages take more than 3 seconds to show content, visitors leave.
  2. Traffic but no inquiries. People arrive and nobody calls, fills a form or messages.
  3. It looks dated next to competitors. Visitors judge trust in seconds.
  4. You can't update it yourself. Every price or photo change needs a developer.
  5. It doesn't rank for your services. Searching "your service + your city" doesn't show you.
  6. No clear next step. No visible WhatsApp, call or booking button.
  7. It describes a business you no longer are. New services, new location, new audience.

When does a website not need a redesign?

If the site loads fast, brings steady inquiries and only has one or two weak spots, fix those instead. Common targeted fixes:

ProblemFix instead of redesign
Few inquiriesAdd WhatsApp button, rewrite headline, add reviews
Not rankingService pages, titles, Google Business Profile
Slightly slowCompress images, remove heavy plugins
Outdated contentRewrite copy and replace photos

The copy side is covered in how to write website content that converts.

How do you check your website in 15 minutes?

  • Run your homepage through Google PageSpeed Insights on mobile.
  • Open the site on your phone and try to contact yourself in under 10 seconds.
  • Search your main service plus your city in an incognito window.
  • Check Google Search Console or analytics for visits vs inquiries last month.
  • Open two competitors' sites side by side with yours.

If three or more checks fail, a redesign is likely the better investment.

What should a redesign keep so you don't lose rankings?

Keep URLs that already rank, or set up 301 redirects from old to new addresses. Keep page titles and content that bring traffic, resubmit the sitemap to Search Console after launch, and monitor rankings for a few weeks. Skipping redirects is the most common way a redesign wipes out years of Google traffic. The post-launch steps are in what happens after a website launch.

How much does a website redesign cost?

In India, a small business redesign usually costs about the same as a new site of that size: roughly ₹15,000–₹75,000 for most service businesses, more with custom features. See business website cost in India for a breakdown.

FAQ

Questions about this topic

How do I know if my website needs a redesign?

Common signs are slow mobile loading, few inquiries despite traffic, a dated look compared to competitors, difficulty updating content, poor rankings for your services, no clear call to action, and outdated business information.

How often should a business website be redesigned?

There's no fixed schedule. Many business sites need a redesign every 3–5 years, but only when performance, conversions or the business itself has changed enough to justify it.

Will redesigning my website hurt SEO?

It can if URLs change without 301 redirects or ranking content is removed. With redirects, preserved content and a resubmitted sitemap, rankings usually hold or improve.

Is it better to fix or rebuild an old website?

Fix it if only one or two areas are weak and the site is fast and converting. Rebuild if it fails on speed, mobile, conversions and editability together.
