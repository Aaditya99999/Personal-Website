How Much Does AI Automation Cost for a Small Business in India in 2026?

AI automation for a small business in India in 2026 typically costs between ₹15,000 and ₹1,00,000+, depending on how many steps the workflow has and whether it uses a simple rule-based flow or a full AI agent. Here's the honest breakdown by tier.

AI AutomationIndia 2026Pricing Breakdown

AI automation for a small business in India in 2026 typically costs somewhere between ₹15,000 and ₹1,00,000+. The gap is wide because "AI automation" covers everything from a single WhatsApp auto-reply to a full AI agent that qualifies leads, checks your calendar, and follows up on its own. I build these under AB Labs alongside websites, and this is now the second most common question after "website banane mein kitna lagega" — so here's the same honest, tiered breakdown for automation.

Why AI automation pricing varies so much

Search "AI automation cost India" and you'll see numbers from ₹5,000 to lakhs. Both are true for different things. A ₹5,000 quote is usually a single Zapier-style trigger someone copied from a template. A lakh-plus quote is usually an enterprise AI agent handling multiple departments. For a clinic, a shop, or a solo service provider, the realistic range is much narrower — here's how I break it down.

1. Single workflow automation — ₹15,000 to ₹30,000

One clear automation, built and tested properly. This is the right starting point for most small businesses.

  • One workflow: e.g. website form → WhatsApp/email routing, or a WhatsApp auto-reply for common questions
  • Built on n8n (self-hosted or cloud), so you own the workflow — no lock-in
  • Basic error handling so a failed message doesn't just disappear
  • Turnaround: usually 3-5 days

2. Multi-step automation — ₹30,000 to ₹60,000

This connects several steps together so a lead moves through stages without you touching a spreadsheet.

  • Lead capture → qualification questions → WhatsApp follow-up sequence
  • Appointment booking synced to a calendar
  • Simple dashboard or sheet showing every inquiry and its status
  • Turnaround: usually 1-2 weeks

3. AI agent automation — ₹60,000 to ₹1,00,000+

This is where automation stops following a fixed script and starts reasoning. I build this tier for clinics and businesses with a high volume of repetitive but varied questions.

  • Everything in tier 2
  • A conversational AI agent (via WhatsApp or web chat) that can answer questions from your actual business documents, not a generic script
  • Handoff to a human when the agent hits something it can't answer confidently
  • Multi-channel: WhatsApp, email, and web chat feeding the same system

For a clinic client I work with, the ask wasn't just "add a chatbot" — it was "make sure the same 15 questions parents ask every day stop landing in my personal WhatsApp at midnight." That's an AI agent problem, not a simple workflow, and it changes both scope and cost. See the EMR case study for the kind of build this connects to.

If a quote doesn't mention which of these applies, ask — a ₹15,000 automation and a ₹70,000 automation can both be called "AI automation," but they solve very different problems.

A quick gut check before you pay for anything

Before committing to a price, ask three questions:

  1. Does this run on a tool I actually own (like n8n), or is it locked to the agency's account?
  2. What happens when the automation fails — does the message just vanish, or is there a fallback?
  3. What's the realistic monthly cost after launch, not just the build cost?

If those answers are vague, that's a bigger warning sign than the number itself. See the full automation services page for what's typically included at each tier.

What I'd actually recommend

If you're getting a steady but manageable stream of inquiries: start with single workflow automation. It's the tier that removes the most obvious pain (missed WhatsApp messages) without paying for reasoning you don't need yet. If you're fielding the same 10-20 questions daily and losing hours to it, the AI agent tier usually pays for itself within a couple of months.

FAQ

Questions about this topic

What's the cheapest AI automation setup for a small business?

A single WhatsApp auto-reply or form-to-WhatsApp routing workflow starts around ₹15,000, covering one clear automation with basic error handling.

Is n8n free to use for automation?

n8n itself is open-source and free to self-host, but you still pay for hosting, setup time, and any paid AI models the workflow calls — that build and running cost is what you're actually quoted.

Does AI automation replace the need for a website?

No — automation works best connected to a website or existing contact channel. It routes and responds to inquiries; it doesn't replace the page that explains your business and builds trust.

How long does it take to build an AI automation workflow?

A single-workflow build usually takes 3-5 days. Multi-step automation with an AI agent typically takes 1-3 weeks depending on how many tools it needs to connect to.

Do I need to keep paying after the automation is built?

Usually yes, in small amounts — hosting for the workflow engine and API costs for any AI model calls. For most small businesses this runs ₹500-₹3,000/month depending on volume.
