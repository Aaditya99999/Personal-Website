Does Your Restaurant Actually Need a Website With Online Ordering?

Relying only on Zomato and Swiggy means paying heavy commissions on every order. A website with a menu, hours, location, and WhatsApp/direct ordering captures repeat customers without the cut.

AB Labs6 min readPublished July 26, 2026
RestaurantsOnline OrderingCommissions

Zomato and Swiggy solve discovery — they put your restaurant in front of people searching for food nearby. What they don't solve is margin: commissions typically run 20–30% per order, which quietly erodes profit on every single sale, including from customers who already know and like you. A website with a menu, hours, location, and a direct WhatsApp or call-in ordering option gives repeat customers a way to order without you paying that cut every time.

💡

Quick answer: Keep Zomato/Swiggy for discovery, but add a website with your menu and a WhatsApp ordering link so repeat customers can order directly — no commission, and you keep their contact for future promotions.

Why not just rely on Zomato/Swiggy entirely?

They're genuinely useful for new customer discovery, and dropping them isn't usually the right move. The issue is exclusivity — if every order, even from a regular customer who already has your number saved, goes through the app, you're paying commission on demand you already earned. A website doesn't compete with the apps; it captures the portion of demand that doesn't need to go through them.

What actually goes on the website

  • Full menu with prices: updated, categorized, and easy to scan on a phone screen.
  • Location and hours: with a map embed and clear timing, including holiday exceptions.
  • WhatsApp ordering link: pre-filled with a starter message like "Hi, I'd like to order for pickup/delivery."
  • Reviews: pulled from Google, displayed prominently — social proof matters as much for restaurants as for any other local business.

"Every order that goes through a delivery app instead of directly to you is margin you already earned, quietly given away."

Does a restaurant need online payment integration?

Not necessarily to start. Many restaurants begin with WhatsApp ordering and cash/UPI on delivery or pickup — simple, low-setup, and enough to capture direct orders immediately. Full online payment integration is a reasonable next step once direct order volume justifies the added complexity, but it's not a blocker to getting started.

The simplest version to start with

You don't need a full online-ordering system on day one. A single page with your menu, hours, location, and a WhatsApp ordering button is enough to start capturing direct orders. From there, the same automation ideas in how to automate WhatsApp Business replies apply directly — an automatic order confirmation, and a simple way to track repeat customers.

Getting found by new customers too

A website also helps you get found on Google directly, not just inside delivery apps — see Google Business Profile + website for how the two work together for any local food business.

A short checklist

  1. Is the menu on your website current and priced correctly?
  2. Is there a WhatsApp ordering link, not just a phone number?
  3. Are hours and location visible without scrolling?
  4. Are you capturing repeat customers' WhatsApp numbers for future offers?
  5. Does the site load fast on mobile, where most food searches happen?
