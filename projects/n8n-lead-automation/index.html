Automation Case Study

Website Lead to WhatsApp and CRM Automation

A practical automation setup for businesses that want to stop losing leads because of slow response and manual admin work.

Client type

Small business or service provider collecting inquiries from a website.

Problem

Leads arrive through a form, but response time is inconsistent and the data does not always get stored cleanly.

Solution

Use n8n to route each form submission into a WhatsApp alert, a tracking sheet or CRM, and a simple follow-up action.

Tools used

Website form, WhatsApp notifications, Google Sheets or CRM destination, and n8n as the workflow engine.

Features

  • Instant lead alert
  • Automatic record creation
  • Cleaner internal lead visibility
  • Reduced missed follow-up

Outcome

This type of workflow helps businesses respond faster and stay more organized without adding a lot of extra software complexity.
