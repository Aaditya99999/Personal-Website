Digital Prescription System

A concept for clinics and doctors who want more structured patient records and a cleaner prescription workflow.

Client type

Doctor or clinic handling repeat patients and manual prescription documentation.

Problem

Manual prescriptions and fragmented records take time and make repeat visits harder to manage cleanly.

Solution

A digital workflow that stores patient history, prepares consistent prescription output, and supports record retrieval and sharing.

Tools used

Structured form capture, workflow automation, and PDF output concepts for repeatable clinic processes.

Features

  • Patient details capture
  • Prescription record creation
  • PDF-style prescription output
  • History support for repeat visits

Outcome

This type of system helps clinics reduce repetitive work and creates a more organized operational foundation for growth.
