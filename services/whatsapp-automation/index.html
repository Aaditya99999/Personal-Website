WhatsApp Automation for Business

WhatsApp Automation for Small Businesses

If your business gets inquiries on WhatsApp, you should not be relying on slow manual replies alone. I build systems that acknowledge leads, confirm bookings, send reminders, and keep teams updated.

Use cases

  • Instant response to website leads
  • Appointment confirmations and reminders
  • New inquiry alerts to the owner or team
  • Basic follow-up flows after form submission

What gets connected

  • Website forms
  • Google Sheets or CRM tools
  • Calendar and booking workflows
  • Email or internal team notifications

Why this matters

Many small businesses lose leads simply because response time is inconsistent. WhatsApp automation improves the first response and reduces missed opportunities.

Faster response

Leads know the inquiry was received right away.

Better booking flow

Confirm and remind people without handling each message manually.

Cleaner follow-up

Track or route important inquiries into your other tools.

Can this connect to my website?

Yes. A common setup is form submission to WhatsApp acknowledgement plus a backend lead log.

Can this work for clinics or coaches?

Yes. Appointment confirmations and reminder messages are strong use cases for both.

Can we start small?

Yes. The best approach is to automate one high-value WhatsApp flow first.

What is the budget?

Basic WhatsApp automation setups usually fit within the broader automation starting range under Rs 20,000.
