About the Academy

Built for Serious Market Learners in Kota

Kota Algo Stock Academy was started with one clear purpose — to give serious learners a structured, data-backed, and practical way to understand and participate in Indian stock markets.

Our Story
How It All Began

Kota has always been a city of serious learners. Students come here to prepare for some of the toughest examinations in India. We asked ourselves — why shouldn't that same discipline and seriousness apply to understanding financial markets?

Most stock market institutes in India focus on surface-level content — a few chart patterns, some basic theory, and generic tips. That was never enough. Markets are data-driven systems, and trading them well requires a systematic, structured, and analytical approach.

Kota Algo Stock Academy was founded to bridge that gap. We built our own screener tool, structured a curriculum from fundamentals to algorithmic thinking, and started training students the right way — with logic, data, and real market practice.

The market rewards those who understand data and punishes those who follow emotions. Our job is to make sure every student at this academy understands that difference before they ever place a real trade.
Founder, Kota Algo Stock Academy Kota, Rajasthan

The Learning Journey
From First Session to Advanced Trader

We designed a journey that any motivated learner can follow — regardless of whether they come in as a complete beginner or someone with some market experience.

The goal at every stage is simple: build real understanding, not just familiarity. Each module prepares students for the next one, so by the time they reach live market practice, they are genuinely ready.

Stock Market Orientation

Understanding exchanges, instruments, brokers, and how Indian markets are structured. Building the base vocabulary every trader must have.

Technical Concepts & Charting

Candlesticks, chart patterns, volume analysis, moving averages, and key indicators that form the foundation of technical trading.

Screener Tool Training

Learning to use the academy's own screener to filter, rank, and shortlist stocks for analysis — dramatically cutting down study time.

Strategy & Algorithm Thinking

Building rule-based strategies. Understanding what conditions to enter and exit trades. Backtesting using 30 years of data.

Live Market Exposure

Applying everything in real-time during market hours. Learning to handle uncertainty, news, and volatility with a structured mindset.
